The presentation

A previously well 7-year-old boy from a rural area, referred to PICU with 2 days of fever, headache and vomiting, then myalgia and a generalized rash. Referring diagnosis: staphylococcal toxic shock syndrome, on vancomycin. On exam: hypotension, tachycardia, GCS 11, bilateral conjunctival suffusion, calf-muscle tenderness, hepatosplenomegaly, diffuse erythema with intraoral bleeding. Labs show profound hypoglycemia, metabolic acidosis, hyponatremia, hypoalbuminemia, a coagulopathy (PT 30s, D-dimer 7699) — and, notably, normal platelets and normal creatinine.

Problem representation

What this isⓘGusEngine's one-line problem representation — the same abstraction a clinician forms before naming a diagnosis. Everything downstream reasons from this framing, not from the raw fact list.

A young child from a rural area with an acute, severe febrile multisystem illness — conjunctival suffusion, calf tenderness, diffuse erythema with intraoral bleeding, and hepatosplenomegaly — accompanied by profound hypoglycemia, hyponatremia, hypoalbuminemia, and coagulopathy.

Reasoning mode

etiologic discriminationThe kind of reasoning the case calls for — recognizing the syndrome, finding its cause, or confirming a known one. Here the syndrome is clear; the work is which cause, so the output focuses on discriminators rather than re-naming the picture. The register matches the mode.

Physiologic reading — what the findings mean

What this isⓘBefore ranking any diagnosis, GusEngine reads what the findings mean together physiologically — the shared substrate each candidate then has to explain. Diagnosis-independent.

Systemic hypoperfusion and microvascular failure → anaerobic metabolism and a severe metabolic acidosis.HCO₃ 10 · lactate 2.4 · capillary refill 3s

Active skeletal-muscle injury (myositis) → leak of intracellular muscle enzymes.myalgia · calf tenderness · CK 519

A consumptive coagulopathy / DIC → clotting-factor depletion with clinical bleeding.intraoral bleeding · PT 30.2s · D-dimer 7699

Hepatic congestion with capillary leak → impaired biliary excretion and low oncotic pressure.total bilirubin 3.0 · albumin 1.9 · hepatomegaly
